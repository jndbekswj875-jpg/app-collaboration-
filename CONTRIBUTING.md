# Share your experience

[Japanese version](CONTRIBUTING.ja.md)

We are designing a **free-to-use AI-assisted chat workspace** for personal gatherings and communities.

You do not have to be a developer. We would love to understand **what information gets lost when you organize an event and what would make it easier.**

## How to help shape the app

[Share a problem or idea](https://github.com/jndbekswj875-jpg/app-collaboration-/issues/new?template=feedback-en.yml) in a short GitHub feedback post.

Useful things to tell us:
- What kind of group or event is involved?
- How does your group communicate today?
- What gets missed, delayed, repeated, or misunderstood?
- What would you want the shared workspace or AI assistant to clarify?
- Would you try an early version for free?

We may use general themes from public feedback to guide the project. Please share **only what you're comfortable making public**: GitHub Issues are publicly visible and normally require a GitHub account. Do not include private conversations or other people's names or contact details.

## Current availability

This is a concept with a local browser demo, **not a publicly launched multi-person chat service**. We are collecting needs and ideas, not asking for payments.

If you specifically want to collaborate on source code, see the separate [developer guide](docs/DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md). For everyone else, your experience as an organizer or participant is the most valuable starting point.
