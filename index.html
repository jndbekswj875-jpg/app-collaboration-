Still being developed. The demo is a single-browser prototype, not real group messaging or connected AI. No payment or live account is offered.

THE PROBLEM

Messages arrive. But important information doesn't always reach everyone.

When friends organize a trip, meetup or community event, decisions get lost between fast chats, separate notes, slow replies and last-minute updates. We want to close the gap between talking and sharing the same understanding.
